# AssetX Research Knowledge Base

This folder stores curated web research summaries for AssetX Estate.

Use it for market, business, marketing, legal, and property-credit research gathered through Hermes and Tavily.

## File Naming

```text
YYYY-MM-DD-topic-slug.md
```

## Required Sections

- Date researched
- Research purpose
- Executive summary
- Key findings
- Business implications for AssetX Estate
- Marketing or content ideas
- Legal/compliance caveats
- Sources with URLs
- Follow-up questions

## Safety

- Do not store API keys or credentials here.
- Do not store customer PII or private customer financial details here.
- Treat legal research as preliminary until verified by a qualified professional or official source.
